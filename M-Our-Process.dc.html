1 Sourcing
Most cars don't make our lot.

We buy at dealer auctions and from local sellers — one-owner, clean-history cars in the $10–25k range Miami actually drives. Before we bid, the history report gets pulled. After we buy, the car still has to pass inspection or it goes back to auction, at our loss, not yours.

REJECTED AT THE GATE — NO EXCEPTIONS POLICY
✕Flood or water damage — title-flagged or found physically. We're in a hurricane state; this is check #1.
✕Salvage, rebuilt, or washed titles — including out-of-state re-titles hiding a brand.
✕Frame or structural repair — cosmetic bodywork is fine; bent metal isn't.
✕Odometer discrepancies — mileage that doesn't match the service records.

4 Pricing
Priced to market. Quoted out-the-door.

6 Financing
Two ways to finance. One honest worksheet.

Whichever route you take, the worksheet shows the price, your real APR, the term, and the payment — nothing packed in. Bring your own bank's approval if you like; we'll work with it.

ROUTE A — BANK & CREDIT-UNION PARTNERS
Good-to-strong credit. We shop your application to local lenders and show you the best 2–3 approvals side by side — rate, term, payment.
ROUTE B — IN-HOUSE FINANCING
Thin file, rebuilding, or the banks said no. We finance directly: proof of income + residence, a reasonable down payment, and on-time payments reported so your credit rebuilds.
