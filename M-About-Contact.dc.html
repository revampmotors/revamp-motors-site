About Revamp Motors
Built by people who got burned buying used cars.

Revamp Motors started in 2023 with a simple bet: in a city famous for shady lots, the dealership that shows its work wins. So we publish the inspection, print the fix list, quote the out-the-door number before you visit, and put the warranty in writing. Two years in — 4.9 stars, most of our cars selling inside three weeks — the bet is paying off.
